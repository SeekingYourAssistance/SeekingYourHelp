My Story

Why I’m asking — in my own words

My name is Jubail B. Mohammad. I am 46 years old, a husband and a father of six children — five boys and one girl — and I also care for my elderly father here in Jolo, Sulu.

Before the pandemic, I became ill and needed treatment for a colon condition. Since then, I have been unable to do heavy work. We once had a small source of income, but during the pandemic, our savings and business funds were used up to cover our daily needs and my medicine.

What breaks my heart the most is that four of my children have had to stop going to school, because I can no longer afford their expenses. My father is too old to work, and my wife is unable to work while caring for our youngest children.

No minimum, and no target you must complete. Any amount — even the very smallest — is received with the same sincere thank you.

Livelihood assistance and daily needs only: food and groceries for my family of nine, medicine for my colon condition, and school expenses so my four children can return to their studies. Nothing else.

Will I get anything in return?

No. Nothing is promised or guaranteed in return — no rewards, prizes, matching, multiplication, or repayment. Please give only if you are comfortable giving freely.
